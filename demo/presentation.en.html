🚀 User Flow

Step 1: Install the app
Step 2: Tap My Number Card against smartphone
Step 3: Verify signature with government public key
Step 4: Determine if 18+ or underage & store result
Step 5: Use age verification on Netflix, etc.
Step 6: Issue NFT (approx. $0.07)
Step 7: SaaS company begins service delivery
